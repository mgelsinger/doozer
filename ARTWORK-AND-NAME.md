## What the MIT licence does not cover

- **The creature.** Doozer the character: its design and its likeness, in any form and wherever it is drawn in this repository. That includes the 2D drawing (`app/src/character/`, `mockups/shared/`), the 3D model (`app/assets/doozer/`) and the icons (`app/assets/icon/`, `app/src-tauri/icons/`).
- **The pictures.** The screenshots and other pictures of Doozer, in `docs/screenshots/` and wherever else they are.
- **The name.** "Doozer", as the name of this program and of its creature.

Some files are code and drawing at once: the ones that draw and move the creature. The code in them is under the MIT licence, and you may learn from it and reuse how it works. The creature they draw is not.

## What that means

- **Use the code freely.** Read it, run it, change it, build your own thing on it, sell that if you like. The MIT licence asks only that its notice stays with the code.
- **Give what you pass on a creature and a name of its own.** Do not ship the Doozer character, its icons or its pictures in something you pass on, do not call it Doozer, and do not suggest that it is Doozer or comes from Doozer's author.
- **Saying where it came from is fine:** "based on the code of Doozer", with a link.
- **Building Doozer as it is for yourself is fine**, and so is forking this repository to work on it.
- **Doozer as its author publishes it**, the installer under *Releases*, has its own terms: free to use and to pass on unchanged ([LICENSE.txt](app/src-tauri/installer/LICENSE.txt), installed beside the program).

For anything else, ask: open an issue in this repository.
